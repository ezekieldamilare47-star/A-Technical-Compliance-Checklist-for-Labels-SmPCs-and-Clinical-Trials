## The Two-Linguist Standard

ISO 17100:2015 is the international standard for translation services. Among its core requirements are professionally qualified translators and revision of every translation by a second linguist. Espresso Translations has held this certification since 2018. In practice it means no file is delivered after a single pass: one native-speaking specialist translates, a second linguist reviews for accuracy, terminology and completeness, and a final quality check covers formatting, certification and any stamps or seals.
